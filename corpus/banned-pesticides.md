## Summary
Nepal has banned the import, sale and use of these pesticides: chlordane, dieldrin, aldrin, mirex, lindane, phosphamidon, DDT, endrin, heptachlor, BHC, organomercury fungicides and toxaphene (2001); monocrotophos and methyl parathion (2006); endosulfan (2012); phorate (2015); carbofuran, dichlorvos, triazophos, carbaryl, benomyl, carbosulfan, dicofol and the 3 g aluminium phosphide tablet (2019); and chlorpyrifos and paraquat (December 2024). For the 2024 ban, shops may sell old stock for up to two years, but cannot import more. Many of these were banned because they are used in suicide; poison ingestion is the second leading cause of suicide in Nepal. A product sold in a shop is not proof that it is allowed.

## Steps
- Read the active ingredient on the label, not the brand name.
- If it is on this list, do not buy or use it, even if the shop still has stock.
- Store any pesticide locked away from children and from the kitchen.
