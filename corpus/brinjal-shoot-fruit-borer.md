## Summary
The brinjal shoot and fruit borer bores into tender shoots, which wilt and droop, and then into fruit, leaving holes plugged with droppings. Brinjal is picked every few days, so a long waiting period means lost harvests or unsafe ones. Chlorantraniliprole has a 22 day waiting period on brinjal, much longer than its 3 days on tomato. Broflanilide is 1 day and neem (azadirachtin 1% EC) is 3 days. Carbosulfan and chlorpyrifos are also on brinjal labels in India but are banned in Nepal.

## Steps
- Cut and destroy wilted shoots every week, below the bored part.
- Pick and destroy bored fruit; do not sell or feed them to animals in the field.
- Use pheromone traps to monitor and catch males.
- If you spray, pick by the waiting period: you harvest every few days.
