## Summary
Fruit fly maggots rot gourd fruit from inside; caterpillars bore into bitter gourd. Cucumber, sponge gourd and pumpkin have all been destroyed at Kalimati for residue in 2025. Chlorantraniliprole has a 7 day waiting period on bitter gourd; afidopyropen has 5 days on cucumber for whitefly.

## Steps
- Use cue-lure fruit fly traps and change the lure on time.
- Collect fallen and rotting fruit every few days and bury them deep.
- Wrap young bitter gourd fruit in paper or cloth bags where labour allows.
