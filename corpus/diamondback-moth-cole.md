## Summary
Diamondback moth caterpillars are small, green and wriggle backwards when touched; they make holes and windows in cabbage and cauliflower leaves and can ruin the head. It resists many insecticides, so rotating products matters. Cabbage and cauliflower are eaten fresh and often fail residue tests, so the waiting period is the key choice: on cabbage, broflanilide has a 1 day waiting period, chlorantraniliprole 3 days, and chlorfenapyr and chlorfluazuron 7 days.

## Steps
- Check the underside of leaves twice a week.
- Neem (azadirachtin) products work on young caterpillars and aphids.
- Do not spray the same insecticide group twice in a row.
- On heads you will harvest within a week, use only a product whose waiting period ends before harvest.
