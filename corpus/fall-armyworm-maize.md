## Summary
Fall armyworm reached Nepal in May 2019 and the Plant Quarantine and Pesticide Management Centre declared it in August 2019. By 2020 it had reached almost half the districts, from the Terai to the hills. Farmers have reported up to 80% loss in extreme cases; the expected loss without control is 20 to 25% of maize yield. Young larvae scrape leaves and leave windows; older larvae bore into the whorl and leave wet sawdust-like droppings. Look for an inverted Y on the head and four dark spots in a square near the tail.

Parkha has no verified label waiting period for any insecticide on maize, so it does not give a chemical dose for maize. Ask your Agriculture Knowledge Centre for a product registered in Nepal for maize and follow its label.

## Steps
- Walk the field twice a week from emergence; check 20 plants in a W pattern.
- Crush egg masses and young larvae by hand.
- Put a pinch of fine sand, wood ash or soil into infested whorls.
- Use pheromone traps to know when moths arrive.
- Plant early and at the same time as neighbours; late plantings get the worst attack.
- Spray only if damage is spreading, and only a product the Agriculture Knowledge Centre confirms for maize.

- बिरुवा उम्रिएदेखि हप्तामा दुई पटक खेत घुम्नुहोस्; W आकारमा हिँडेर २० बोट जाँच्नुहोस्।
- फुलको थुप्रो र साना लार्भा हातले थिचेर मार्नुहोस्।
- कीरा लागेको गाभामा अलिकति मसिनो बालुवा, खरानी वा माटो हाल्नुहोस्।
- पुतली आएको थाहा पाउन फेरोमोन पासो राख्नुहोस्।
- छिमेकीसँगै समयमै रोप्नुहोस्; ढिला रोपेको मकैमा बढी आक्रमण हुन्छ।
- क्षति फैलिँदै गएमा मात्र, कृषि ज्ञान केन्द्रले मकैका लागि पुष्टि गरेको विषादी मात्र छर्नुहोस्।
