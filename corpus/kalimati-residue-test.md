## Summary
The Rapid Bioassay of Pesticide Residue (RBPR) unit at Kalimati market and the Central Agricultural Laboratory test vegetables for organophosphate and carbamate insecticide residue. Below 35% inhibition the produce is safe; 35 to 45% can be eaten only after a waiting period; above 45% it is destroyed. In October 2025 round chilli from Makawanpur (57%), green pumpkin from Makawanpur (46%), sponge gourd from Dhading (65%) and cucumber from Kathmandu (53%) were destroyed. Earlier in 2025, 600 kg of leafy greens and 32 kg of beans were discarded. The lab now tests 17 to 18 kinds of vegetables daily. Leafy greens, broccoli, tomato and cauliflower often fail.

## Steps
- Most failures are organophosphate or carbamate residue, so these products need the most care with the waiting period.
- Harvest only after the label waiting period has passed.
- Keep your spray record; it helps you and your trader if a lot is questioned.
