## Summary
Fruit borer caterpillars bore into okra pods, chilli and tomato fruit. Mites make chilli leaves curl down and turn leathery. Okra and chilli are picked every two or three days and round chilli has failed residue tests at Kalimati. On chilli, broflanilide has a 1 day waiting period, acetamiprid and chlorantraniliprole 3 days, buprofezin and chlorfenapyr 5 days for mites, and acephate, an organophosphate, 7 days. On okra, chlorantraniliprole is 5 days.

## Steps
- Pick and destroy bored fruit at every harvest.
- Use pheromone traps for fruit borer.
- For mites, spray the underside of leaves; mites are not insects and many insecticides do not work on them.
- Prefer a product whose waiting period is shorter than your picking interval.
