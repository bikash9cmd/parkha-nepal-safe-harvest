## Summary
Stem borer larvae kill the central shoot (dead heart) in young rice and leave white empty panicles (white head) later. Leaf folders roll leaves and scrape them white. Rice waiting periods are long: chlorantraniliprole 47 days, bifenthrin 21 days, buprofezin 20 days for planthoppers. Carbofuran and chlorpyrifos granules are on Indian rice labels but are banned in Nepal.

## Steps
- Pull and destroy dead hearts early.
- Do not over-apply nitrogen; soft lush plants attract more borers and planthoppers.
- Drain the field for a few days if planthoppers build up at the base.
- If a spray is needed late in the season, check that its waiting period ends before harvest.
