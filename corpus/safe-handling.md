## Summary
Most pesticide harm in Nepal happens at home: products kept in the house are swallowed by children or by someone in crisis, and sprayers are poisoned through skin and breathing. In hospitals, very few poisoning patients arrive with the bottle, which makes treatment harder.

## Steps
- Wear gloves, a mask, long sleeves and boots; do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying.
- Do not spray in wind or in the heat of the day.
- Do not mix two pesticides in one tank unless the label says so.
- Keep pesticides locked, in the original container, away from food, the kitchen and children.
- Do not reuse empty containers for water or food.
- If someone is poisoned, take them to the nearest hospital immediately and take the bottle or label with you.

## चरणहरू
- पन्जा, मास्क, लामो बाहुला र जुत्ता लगाउनुहोस्; छर्दा खाने, पिउने वा चुरोट नखानुहोस्।
- हावा चलेको बेला वा चर्को घाममा नछर्नुहोस्।
- लेबलमा नलेखेसम्म दुई विषादी एउटै ट्याङ्कीमा नमिसाउनुहोस्।
- विषादी सक्कली भाँडामै, खानेकुरा, भान्छा र बालबालिकाबाट टाढा ताल्चा लगाएर राख्नुहोस्।
- खाली भाँडा पानी वा खानेकुराका लागि फेरि प्रयोग नगर्नुहोस्।
- कसैलाई विष लागेमा तुरुन्तै नजिकको अस्पताल लैजानुहोस् र विषादीको बोतल वा लेबल पनि साथमा लैजानुहोस्।
