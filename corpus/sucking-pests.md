## Summary
Aphids, whitefly, jassids and thrips suck sap; leaves curl, yellow or turn sticky, and some carry virus diseases. Natural enemies such as ladybird beetles control them when broad-spectrum sprays are avoided. Label waiting periods differ: acetamiprid is 7 days on cabbage and 3 days on okra and chilli; afidopyropen is 1 day on brinjal and 5 days on cucumber; buprofezin is 5 days on okra for jassids.

## Steps
- Hang yellow sticky traps for whitefly and aphids.
- Spray plain water hard on aphid colonies, or use a neem product.
- Avoid broad-spectrum sprays that kill ladybird beetles.
- Remove and destroy plants with curled, virus-like leaves early.
