## Summary
Every registered insecticide label gives a waiting period (pre-harvest interval): the number of days between the last spray and the first harvest of that crop. It is set so that the residue on the produce falls below the safe limit. It depends on the product and the crop, not on the pest: chlorantraniliprole is 3 days on tomato but 22 days on brinjal. In a Dhading survey, 56% of vegetable farmers waited only three to five days after spraying, whatever the product. Officials at the Central Agriculture Laboratory say farmers who follow the label dose and the waiting period would bring residues within safe limits.

## Steps
- Write down the date and the product name every time you spray.
- Count the waiting period from the last spray, not the first.
- Pick the vegetables you will sell this week before you spray, and do not spray those plants.
- If two products were used, wait for the longer waiting period.
- Never spray to make vegetables look fresh before market. That is the main reason vegetables fail the test at Kalimati.

## चरणहरू
- हरेक पटक विषादी छर्दा मिति र विषादीको नाम लेखेर राख्नुहोस्।
- पर्खने अवधि अन्तिम पटक छरेको दिनदेखि गन्नुहोस्।
- यो हप्ता बेच्ने तरकारी छर्नुअघि नै छुट्याउनुहोस्, ती बोटमा नछर्नुहोस्।
- दुई किसिमका विषादी प्रयोग गर्नुभएको छ भने लामो पर्खने अवधि पालना गर्नुहोस्।
- बजार लैजानुअघि तरकारी ताजा देखाउन कहिल्यै विषादी नछर्नुहोस्। कालीमाटीमा तरकारी परीक्षणमा फेल हुने मुख्य कारण यही हो।
