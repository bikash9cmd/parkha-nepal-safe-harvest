## Summary
Every district has an Agriculture Knowledge Centre (कृषि ज्ञान केन्द्र) under the provincial government, and municipalities have agriculture sections with technicians who can visit fields and confirm which products are registered in Nepal. For anyone thinking about harming themselves, the national suicide prevention helpline is 1166.

## Steps
- Take a clear photo of the damage and a sample in a sealed bag to the Agriculture Knowledge Centre or your municipality's agriculture section.
- Ask for a product registered in Nepal for your crop and pest, and its waiting period.
